Understanding Artificial Intelligence

Mission status: Active

What is artificial intelligence?

Artificial intelligence, usually shortened to AI, is the simulation of human intelligence by machines.

AI systems can learn, reason, solve problems and understand language.

As a student, AI can support your learning by providing more personalised learning experiences, helping with some administrative tasks and complementing your research skills.

AI can also help you explore difficult theories and subjects. For example, it can break complex concepts down into simpler, easier-to-understand explanations.

Used appropriately, AI can make learning new knowledge and skills more engaging and enjoyable.
