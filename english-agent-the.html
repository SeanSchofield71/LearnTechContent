SURVEILLANCE REPORT

At 21:35, the individual known as Morgan left the building carrying a small black case. The case remained secured to Morgan throughout the observation period.

Morgan then moved towards the northbound platforms.

At 22:14, surveillance was abandoned after the station's security staff began paying attention to the field team.

Morgan was still carrying the black case when visual contact was lost.
